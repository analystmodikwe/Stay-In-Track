# STAY IN TRACK — Complete Prompts & Screen Directory
**Project:** STAY IN TRACK — Gym Membership & Payment Tracking System  
**Description:** Full catalog of generation specifications and prompts utilized across all screens and pop-up modals.

---

## 1. Style Guide & Component Foundations
- **Prompt:**
  > "Create a mobile-first style guide and component sheet for a web app called 'STAY IN TRACK' (gym membership payment tracking web app). Colors: Inter font, navy #1F3A5F primary, teal #0F766E accent, neutral background #F5F7FA, 8px spacing grid, 16px side margins, 48px button touch targets, 8px input radius, Lucide outline icons, no dark mode. Tone of voice is short and respectful, like a helpful front-desk colleague."

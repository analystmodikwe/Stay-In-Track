Privacy Notice

STAY IN TRACK

Your data privacy at Iron Haven Gym

STAY IN TRACK is built to help your gym verify active membership and record in-person or EFT payments simply and safely.

What we collect

We collect your name, email and, for Members, phone number and Payment records, so your gym can manage memberships.

Why

We use this information to confirm your active gym floor access, track your renewal dates, and send optional payment reminders.

Who can see it

Only authorized gym administrators and front-desk staff can see your member profile and fee status. We never sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Your data is encrypted and securely hosted on protected cloud servers with strict backup and access safeguards.

How long we keep it

We retain records while your gym membership is active and for required accounting cycles so your payment receipts remain auditable.

How to delete it

You can delete your login account at any time in Settings.

Important retention note

When a Member deletes their account, the gym keeps the Member record and Payment records until the Admin deletes them.

Have questions about your records?

Speak to your front-desk manager at Iron Haven Gym or email support@ironhaven.example.com
